About Us

With a passion for creativity and attention to detail, we specialize in bridal makeup, special event looks, and traditional styles. Whether it’s your wedding day, a celebration, or a cultural event, our goal is to create a flawless finish that complements your features and reflects your personality.

Every client experience at Nikki Beauty Maison is tailored with care, ensuring you feel comfortable, confident, and radiant from the moment you sit in our chair. Using professional techniques and quality products, we deliver long-lasting, elegant looks designed to make unforgettable moments even more beautiful.

Based in Plumstead, London, we proudly serve clients who value professionalism, artistry, and timeless beauty.

Do you offer trial sessions?

+

Yes! We highly recommend trial sessions before your big event. This allows us to understand your preferences and ensure you're completely happy with the look.

Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

+

We use professional-grade, hypoallergenic products suitable for all skin types. We can always adjust based on your skin's needs and any allergies you may have.

What's your cancellation policy?

+

We require 48 hours notice for cancellations. Your £20 deposit is non-refundable but can be transferred to another date if requested in advance.

Do you offer bridal party makeup?

+

Absolutely! We offer group bookings for bridesmaids, mothers, and other wedding party members. We provide coordinated looks while maintaining each person's unique features.
