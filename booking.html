BOOKING & SALON POLICY

Deposits

A non-refundable deposit of £20 is required to secure all bookings. All deposits are NON-REFUNDABLE under any circumstances.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

At least 48 hours' notice is required for cancellations or rescheduling. Deposits may be transferred to a rescheduled appointment if this notice is given. If less than 48 hours' notice is given, the deposit will be lost.

Lateness

  • • Please arrive on time to avoid disruption.
  • • 10 minutes late: £10 fee added.
  • • 15 minutes late: £15 fee added.
  • • 30 minutes or more: Appointment cancelled and deposit forfeited.

No-Shows

Failure to attend your appointment will result in full loss of your deposit.
